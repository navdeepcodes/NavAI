Hershey Script (Simplex) — pen-stroke letterforms used for Mike's handwriting.

The Hershey fonts were created by Dr. A. V. Hershey at the U.S. National
Bureau of Standards (1967) and are in the public domain. The stroke data in
hershey_script.json was extracted from the "scripts" font as distributed in
the Hershey-Fonts Python package (MIT License, https://github.com/apshu/HersheyFonts).
